See what’s free before you plan the drive

Pick your nights and who’s coming. Each campground you can book online shows how many sites are free for your whole stay, so you can weigh up Conto against Boranup without clicking through each one.

Narrow it down by region, by facilities such as toilets or dogs permitted, or to the places you can book online.

Every site, night by night

Open a campground to see each site on each night of your stay, with its price, how many people and vehicles it takes, and what the site is like.

Closures and campground rules sit at the top, and the campground map is a click away.

Booked out? Set a watch

Tell WA Stay where you’d like to stay and when. It checks as often as every 15 minutes and lets you know if a site frees up, with a desktop notification or an email from your own email account.

It can also hold the site as soon as it finds one. ParkStay then keeps it for you for 30 minutes while you pay.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. WA Stay is free and open source, with no ads, subscription or in-app purchases. You pay campground fees to ParkStay, as you would on its website.

Does WA Stay book sites for me?

No. It finds sites, and a watch can hold one for you, but you finish the booking on ParkStay’s own pages. Card details go into ParkStay’s payment page; WA Stay doesn’t read or keep them.

Do I need an account?

There’s no WA Stay account to make. You don’t need a ParkStay account to search or set watches either. ParkStay may ask you to sign in when you pay, as it does on its website, and signing in from WA Stay’s settings beforehand makes paying quicker.

Will it hold more than one site at a time?

Not for the same night. WA Stay never holds more than one site per night across all your watches, in line with ParkStay’s terms.

Does it keep checking when my computer is off?

No. WA Stay checks your watches while it’s open and your computer is awake. In its settings you can have it start when you sign in to Windows, minimised to the taskbar.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is that OK?

WA Stay isn’t code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time you run the installer. Choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Is there a Mac version?

No. WA Stay is for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

Where does my information go?

Your watches, settings and email details stay on your computer. WA Stay talks to ParkStay for campgrounds and availability, to Mapbox for the map (which also sends Mapbox its standard usage data), to GitHub to check for updates, and to your email provider if you turn on email alerts. WA Stay itself has no analytics.

Is WA Stay part of ParkStay WA?

No. WA Stay is an independent app that works with ParkStay’s public booking system. It isn’t made by or affiliated with DBCA, which runs ParkStay WA.

I use WA ParkStay Bookings. What happens to it?

WA Stay is its new name. Your copy will offer the update, and your watches, notifications and settings come with you.
