# WA Stay brand

The WA Stay logo, as generated by `scripts/brand/logo.js` (`npm run icons`). These SVGs are the source of truth for every icon, installer image and app copy. Do not edit them by hand: the pipeline, sizes, usage rules, palette and licence are in [`../README.md`](../README.md).

Not affiliated with Tourism WA or DBCA.

## Concepts (BQ1)

Three original concepts were drawn. `roofline` ships; the others stay in `concepts/` and can be swapped in by changing `CONCEPT` in `scripts/brand/logo.js`.

| Concept | Idea | At 16 px |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Roofline sunset** (`roofline`, shipping) | A gold sun sets into an Indian Ocean brushstroke under an ink roofline. The chevron is a place to stay and the A of WA, with the stroke as its crossbar. | Ink chevron over a blue bar, clear on sand and on a dark taskbar |
| Sun tent (`sun-tent`) | The setting sun is also a dome tent, its door open onto the stroke. | Clear, but reads as camping only |
| Painted monogram (`monogram`) | "WA" in Fraunces reversed out of one loaded stroke of ocean, the sun going down behind it. | The letters blur together |

Why Roofline sunset: it holds a crisp silhouette at 16 px on both light and dark taskbars; the roof-plus-A reading is specific to WA Stay and covers every kind of stay, not just camping; the sunset over the Indian Ocean is the WA moment; and one real painted stroke against flat geometric shapes looks made by hand rather than generated. Sun tent puts a gold half disc on a blue stroke at the centre of the mark, which sits closer to the reference image's sun than we want.

## Contrast

WCAG 2.x ratios, computed from `src/renderer/styles/tokens.css` (a test recomputes them and fails if these numbers drift). Graphics need 3:1 against what they sit on.

| Element | On | Ratio | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lockup ink (`ink-900`: chevron and wordmark) | sand (`sand-50`) | 16.65:1 | 3:1 |
| Lockup ink (`ink-900`) | white (`sand-0`) | 17.79:1 | 3:1 |
| Mono variant in `sand-0` (fg-inverse), reversed | ocean blue (`ocean-500`) | 4.81:1 | 3:1 |
| Mono variant in `sand-0`, reversed | brand (`ocean-600`) | 6.38:1 | 3:1 |
| Mono variant in `ink-900` | ocean blue (`ocean-500`) | 3.70:1 | 3:1 |
| Ocean stroke (`ocean-500`) | the sand plate (`sand-50`) | 4.50:1 | 3:1 |

The sun (`sun-400`) and the coral glint (`coral-400`) are decorative. The sun is 1.72:1 on sand, so it always sits inside the chevron, which carries the shape. The glint sits on the ocean stroke (1.17:1); it is a warm hue accent and nothing depends on seeing it.
